Bird Migration in Australia

Exploring the seasonal patterns, population trends, and geographic spread of migratory shorebirds across Australia.

Australia's Most Sighted Birds

Australia is home to hundreds of bird species. These charts highlight the most frequently sighted birds and the species most consistently reported by birdwatchers across the country.

Shorebird Migration Patterns Over Time

Migratory shorebirds travel thousands of kilometres each year between their breeding grounds in Siberia and Alaska and their non-breeding grounds in Australia. Use the brush below the line chart to zoom into specific years, and click the legend to highlight individual species.

Where Do Shorebirds Gather?

Western Australia consistently records the highest shorebird counts in the country, driven by internationally significant sites such as Eighty Mile Beach. The scatter plot and bubble chart below reveal how individual sites and states compare against national thresholds.

A Local Lens: Bird Sightings in Greater Geelong

Zooming into Greater Geelong, citizen science records reveal rich biodiversity across the region. Use the species filter on the map to explore where individual species have been spotted, and the brush on the area chart to examine sighting trends over time.
